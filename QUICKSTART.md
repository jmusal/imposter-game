# Quick Start Guide

## For Your Daughter & Her Friends

### First Time Setup (On Your Computer)

1. Open a terminal and navigate to the project:
   ```bash
   cd /var/home/jmusal/imposter-game
   ```

2. Start the development server:
   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```

3. Open your browser to **http://localhost:5173**

4. Test the game by clicking through a full round

### Install on Phone

1. **iPhone**: Open Safari, tap the share icon, tap "Add to Home Screen"
2. **Android**: Open Chrome, tap the menu (⋮), tap "Install app"
3. Give it a name (e.g., "Imposter") and tap Install

### Sharing with Friends

Once installed on a phone, it's ready to play! No internet needed (after first load).

## How to Play (Simple Version)

1. **Setup Screen**: 
   - Set players to your group size (e.g., 5)
   - Imposters = usually 1-2
   - Duration = 5 minutes is good (can adjust)
   - Pick a word category

2. **Pass the Phone**:
   - Hand phone to Player 1 → tap to see their role
   - Hand phone to Player 2 → tap to see their role
   - Repeat for all players

3. **Clue Time** (5 minute timer):
   - Each player says one word that relates to the secret word
   - Crewmates want to give helpful hints
   - Imposters want to blend in
   - Use the app to record clues if you like

4. **Voting**:
   - Everyone votes for who's the imposter
   - App shows results

5. **Play Again**:
   - Tap "Play Again", pick new category, repeat!

## Scoring

- **Crewmates** get 1 point for voting out imposters
- **Imposters** get 1 point for surviving or tricking crewmates
- Scores persist across rounds in the app

## Tips

- **For Crewmates**: Give specific clues, not just "food" or "animal"
  - Good: "raw" (for Sushi), "tube" (for Television)
  - Bad: "food", "electronic"

- **For Imposters**: Listen carefully to others' clues to figure out the word
  - Ask careful questions
  - Don't give obviously wrong clues
  - Stay quiet if you're unsure

- **For Everyone**: Watch people's reactions. Who's confident? Who's struggling?

## Troubleshooting

**"Can't see the word on the Role Reveal screen?"**
- Make sure you're looking at YOUR player's phone, not someone else's
- The screen shows different content for crewmates vs imposters

**"Timer ran out too fast/slow?"**
- You can adjust the round duration on the Setup Screen (1-10 minutes)

**"Want to restart the game?"**
- You'll get a "Play Again" button after each round that resets everything

## Enjoy! 🎉

That's it. Have fun with your daughter and her friends!
